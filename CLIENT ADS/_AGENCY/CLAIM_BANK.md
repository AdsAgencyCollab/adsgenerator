## The standing vial label

    PeptideAds
    META ADS
    FOR PEPTIDE BRANDS ONLY

The third line is **FOR PEPTIDE BRANDS ONLY**. An earlier render used
`FOR AGENCY USE ONLY`, which was invented here and is wrong. The launched
winners use the line above.

A metric from L1–L12 may sit on the vial in place of, or beneath, `META ADS`
when the concept calls for it. Some concepts carry no metric at all — that is
a per-ad decision, not a default.

## Rule

An ad carries numbers from this page or it carries none. No revenue figures
outside L1–L12, no ROAS framed as a promise of outcome, no testimonials, no
scarcity or capacity counts, no dates, no countdowns.
