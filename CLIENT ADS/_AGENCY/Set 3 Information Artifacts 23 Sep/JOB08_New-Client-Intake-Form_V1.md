## The placement rule, now stated properly

This job is the cleanest evidence yet, because it differs from job 7 in exactly
one way: **job 7 printed its footer on the card; job 8 printed its footer on the
bare desk below the sheet.**

| job | footer sits on | briefed | landed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | the board | — | 74.8% |
| 7 | the card | 71% | 66–70% |
| 6 | the floor | 77% | 91.8–94.1% |
| 8 | the desk | 77% | **92.8–95.4%** |

**Type printed on the object stays where it is put. Type placed on the ground
below the object migrates to the bottom of the frame, every time.** That is the
rule, and it is now four for four. Put every readable element on the artifact.

## The top margin trick did not work

I added a deep bare-paper band to the 9:16 prompts — sheet top edge at 11%, first
printed mark at 15%, plus `no text above 15 percent` in the negative list — to try
to fix job 7's top breach. It moved the masthead barely at all:

| | job 7 | job 8 take 1 | job 8 take 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| first line top | 11.2% | 12.5% | 13.0% |

Still inside Meta's Reels top-14% band. An instruction to leave a margin is just
another restated percentage. **Move the object's top edge down to about 16% and
let the physics do it** — same as the bottom fix. That is untested and is the one
thing to try on the next vertical.
